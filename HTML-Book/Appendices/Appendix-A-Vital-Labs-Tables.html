A.5 WHO Growth-Chart Use (CH4, CH20.6)

  • Sex-specific WHO charts: weight-for-age, length/height-for-age, head-circumference-for-age, weight-for-length/height, and BMI-for-age.
  • Plot each measurement; use the same chart type every visit; interpret by percentile and z-score.
  • Flags: crossing >2 major percentile lines, drop >1 z-score, weight-for-length <−2SD (wasting), height-for-age <−2SD (stunting), MUAC <11.5 cm (6–59 mo).
  • WHO MUAC screening (6–59 mo): ≥12.5 cm normal; 11.5–12.4 cm moderate; <11.5 cm severe acute malnutrition.
