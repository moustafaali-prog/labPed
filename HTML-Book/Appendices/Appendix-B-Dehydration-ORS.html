B.2 ORS Composition and Volumes

  • ORS: 75 mmol/L sodium + 13.5 g/L glucose; tap water, juice, soup, or broth are not rehydration fluids.
  • Plan A (no dehyd.): and continue feeding.
  • Plan B — mild: 50 mL/kg over 4 h. Moderate: 100 mL/kg over 4 h — give 5–10 mL by spoon/syringe every 2–5 min; vomiting is not a contraindication.
  • Stool-loss replacement: ~10 mL/kg ORS per diarrheal stool.
  • Plan C — severe: IV lactated Ringer 40 mL/kg (bolus 20 mL/kg then reassess), then ORS once stable.
  • Continue breastfeeding throughout; reassess every 4–8 h, weigh daily.
