Appendix C — worked calculation matrices and safe-dose checks (complements CH7, CH8, Procedure 20.13).

C.1 Formulas

CalculationFormula
Dose volumeVolume (mL) = ordered dose (mg) ÷ concentration (mg/mL)
IV ratemL/h = volume (mL) ÷ time (h)
Drip rategtt/min = (mL × drop factor) ÷ time (min)
Weight-based doseOrdered mg/kg/day ÷ frequency = per-administration dose
Maintenance fluidsHolliday–Segar (Appendix A.4)

C.2 Worked Examples

  1. Paracetamol 120 mg PO q6h; suspension 120 mg/5 mL → 120 ÷ (120/5) = 5 mL.
  2. 20 mg/kg/day amoxicillin ÷q8h, child 15 kg → 15 × 20 ÷ 3 = 100 mg q8h; 125 mg/5 mL → 100 ÷ 25 = 4 mL.
  3. 300 mL over 12 h, microdrip 60 gtt/mL → 25 mL/h and 25 gtt/min.
  4. IM ampicillin 200 mg/kg/day ÷q6h, child 12 kg, 500 mg/2 mL → 600 mg q6h = 2.4 mL (recheck vs site-volume limit ≤2 mL).

C.3 Safe-Dose Verification (checklist)

  • Dose within the mg/kg range in the reference; arithmetic rechecked by a second nurse.
  • Volume ≤ site-safe volume (IM infant ≤1 mL; child ≤2 mL).
  • Correct reconstitution/dilution; expiry checked; right route/time; documented.
